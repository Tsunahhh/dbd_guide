## [killers] Universal Builds
### Beginner Build
Perks: Sloppy Butcher (alts: Scourge Hook: Gift of Pain, Brutal Strength) | No Holds Barred | Hex: Plaything (alts: Tinkerer, A Nurse's Calling) | Hex: No One Escapes Death (alts: Enduring)
- Best used on "M1" Killers (who mostly down with their basic attack and move at the usual 4.6m/s speed), like Trapper or Wraith.
- Uses only common perks available to all Killers (with some alternatives from the 5 free ones).
- Use this as a starting point!
### Beginner Build (M2 Killers)
Perks: Scourge Hook: Gift of Pain (alts: Agitation) | No Holds Barred | Spies from the Shadows (alts: Bitter Murmur) | Deerstalker (alts: A Nurse's Calling, Tinkerer)
- Best used on "M2" Killers who mostly rely on their special attack to down, like Nurse or Huntress.
- Uses only common perks available to all Killers (with some alternatives from the 5 free ones).
- Use this as a starting point!
### All-Purpose Simple Build
Perks: Lethal Pursuer (alts: Corrupt Intervention, Discordance) | Barbecue & Chilli (alts: Thrilling Tremors, Tinkerer) | Surge (alts: No Holds Barred, Hex: Ruin) | Grim Embrace (alts: Hex: No One Escapes Death, No Way Out)
- A well-balanced build suitable for any character.,
- Lethal & BBQ will ensure you keep finding Survivors.
- Surge is great for M1-type Killers. Switch to an alternative for M2 Killers!
### Heavy Slowdown Build
Perks: Corrupt Intervention | Scourge Hook: Pain Resonance | Grim Embrace (alts: Surge) | Dead Man's Switch (alts: Eruption)
- Focuses entirely on gen slowdown.
- No info perks, so make sure your tracking is on point.
- Ideal for Killers with built-in detection tools or high mobility.
### Stall & Stealth Build
Perks: Corrupt Intervention (alts: No Holds Barred) | Thrilling Tremors (alts: Grim Embrace) | Secret Project | Barbecue & Chilli (alts: A Nurse's Calling)
- Secret Project grants stealth at the start and throughout the match anytime a gen is blocked.
- Perfect for Killers without built-in stealth abilities that can greatly benefit from ambushing Survivors off-guard.
### Gen-Tracking Build
Perks: Hex: Ruin | Scourge Hook: Pain Resonance | Turn Back the Clock (alts: Hex: Undying, Surge) | Surveillance
- Surveillance constantly turns gens white if they regress and yellow when worked on.
- Your Killer uses only basic attacks? You can try to use Surge to replace one of the regression perks.
- This build works great with Stealth Killers in general.
### Divide & Conquer Build
Perks: Discordance | Tinkerer | No Holds Barred (alts: Turn Back the Clock) | Hex: No One Escapes Death (alts: No Way Out)
- Discordance makes it easier to split Survivors apart so that Tinkerer is easier to act on.
- Even if they split up, they'll never pop too many gens at once due to No Holds Barred.
- Pay attention to the last gen being done so that your endgame perk (NWO or NOED) has a huge impact.
- The info from this build is great on Killers with timed powers like Plague or Oni.
### Oppressive Hex Build
Perks: Hex: Ruin | Hex: Thrill of the Hunt | Hex: Pentimento | Hex: Blood Favour (alts: Hex: Devour Hope, Hex: Undying, Hex: Hive Mind)
- Survivors have to deal with really difficult totems that initially take about 30s to cleanse. If they're unlucky, they might need to cleanse multiple of them before the effects of Hex: Ruin will disappear.
- Any broken totem can be reanimated with Hex: Pentimento, adding a massive slowdown to all Survivors. If Thrill of the Hunt is still active, even the Pentimento totems will take longer to cleanse.
- Pick a 4th Hex perk that suits your Killer and playstyle the best. Hex: Blood Favor is really helpful in chase and Hex: Undying is always a safe choice.
- Does your Killer have an ability or add-on to make Survivors scream from a distance? Bring it with this build and you'll be constantly interrupting their totem cleansing and gen progress.

### Beginner Perks Only
Perks: No Holds Barred | Sloppy Butcher (alts: Scourge Hook: Weeping Wounds) | Agitation (alts: Brutal Strength, Tinkerer, Hex: Fortune's Fool) | Hex: No One Escapes Death

## [killers] The Slasher
### Generic Build
Perks: Scourge Hook: Pain Resonance | Scourge Hook: Jagged Compass (alts: Barbecue & Chilli, Tinkerer, Agitation) | Pop Goes the Weasel (alts: Turn Back the Clock) | Scourge Hook: Weeping Wounds (alts: Scourge Hook: Floods of Rage, Nowhere to Hide, Spies from the Shadows, Agitation, Leverage)
- Reliable and well-rounded build; a good starting point until you find the ideal build for your playstyle.
- Scourge Hook: Jagged Compass will keep creating new Scourge Hooks to guarantee value on your perks, and sometimes point you in the direction of the most progressed generator (which wil be highlighted in yellow). You can then proceed to hit it with one of your regression/gen-blocking perks.
- Make sure that you trigger Pain Res on a Survivor's 1st or 2nd hook, since sometimes Jason kills them directly on the third one and you won't get value from it then. You can also use a non-sacrificial hook spear to ensure that you can pick them up normally instead of triggering the mori.
- Scourge Hook: Weeping Wounds/Gift of Pain will trigger 100% of the time, thanks to Jagged Compass. Floods of Rage is also a great alternative if you need more info. This 4th perk slot is very flexible and can be anything you want.
### Oppressive Hex Build
Perks: Hex: Ruin | Hex: Blood Favour | Hex: Thrill of the Hunt | Hex: Pentimento (alts: Hex: Undying)
- Popularized by Hens, this build brings slowdown and nasty pallet-blocking abilities passively so you can focus on the chase.
- Thrill will keep totems very slow for a long time. Pentimento can relight totems to add even more slowdown and side-objectives.
### Gen-Control Build
Perks: Discordance | Tinkerer | Pop Goes the Weasel (alts: Turn Back the Clock) | Oppression (alts: Call of Brine, Eruption, Machine Learning, No Way Out, Agitation)
- Uses the classic combo of Discordance & Tinkerer to make sure you interrupt grouped Survivors. If they split up on gens, Tinkerer will warn you when a generator is close to being finished so you can damage it with Pop Goes the Weasel.
- Your 4th perk is a flexible choice based on your playstyle!

## [killers] The Judgment
### Generic Build
Perks: Gearhead | A Nurse's Calling (alts: Lethal Pursuer) | Hex: Ruin (alts: No Holds Barred) | Hex: Undying (alts: Corrupt Intervention)
- Reliable and well-rounded build; a good starting point until you find the ideal build for your playstyle.
- Your slowdown from Hex: Ruin can be replaced by other stalling perks that aren't hex-based if you prefer.
- You can also replace one (or both) of your slowdown perks with more aura reading if you want.
- Don't forget to bring one of the aura add-ons like Blind of the Prophet, especially if you're using Lethal Pursuer.
### Hex Trap Build
Perks: Hex: Ruin | Hex: Wretched Fate (alts: Hex: The Third Seal, Hex: Hive Mind, Hex: Thrill of the Hunt) | Hex: Pentimento (alts: Hex: Undying) | Hex: Retribution
- Your first two hexes punish Survivors and slow down their gen progress, however...
- Every time a hex is cleansed, you can relight it with Pentimento to create team-wide gen slowdown.
- On top of that, with Retribution you will reveal the entire team, often letting you find vulnerable Survivors.
### Obsession-Reveal Build
Perks: Nemesis | Celestial Witness | Lethal Pursuer (alts: Hex: Ruin, Rancor) | Corrupt Intervention (alts: No Holds Barred, Remember Me)
- Celestial Witness switches or reveals the Obsession every 30s. 
- Anytime the Obsession changes, Nemesis makes them Oblivious and reveals them for a long time, making them quite vulnerable to being sniped by your pillars.
- Your other perks just provide a bit of slowdown or complement this combo.
### Beginner Perks Only
Perks: No Holds Barred | Deerstalker | Bitter Murmur (alts: Celestial Witness, Tinkerer, A Nurse's Calling) | Hex: No One Escapes Death (alts: Enduring, A Nurse's Calling)
- Only uses perks available to this Killer or global perks available to everyone. It might include some alternative perk ideas from one of the five free Killers too.
- Deerstalker will make you see Survivors that see you with their perks and also reveal you and the Survivor with least chase time every 30s.
- Remember that with Deerstalker, each time it activates the Survivor sees your aura as well! They will often react accordingly.
- Bitter Murmur might give you unexpected info to snipe Survivors and also reveals everyone in the endgame, which could make Hex: NOED really valuable. Ideally, switch to A Nurse's Calling when you can!
