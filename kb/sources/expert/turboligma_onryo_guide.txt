Basic Knowledge/tips You Should Know
### What does locking condemned mean?
This feature was added to incentivise hooking. “Locking” condemned stacks means it's impossible to remove the stacks you had after getting unhooked. As a survivor, if you had 3 stacks and you got hooked, you can’t remove those 3 stacks forever even by inserting tapes. This is counterintuitive because the whole point of condemned moris is to punish survivors that have not been hooked. The max amount that can be locked for the first hook is 3, the second hook is 6.
### Does holding tapes give you condemned ?
Holding a tape allows you to turn off other TVs at the cost of gaining 1 stack. Other than that, holding a tape no longer contributes to gaining stacks in any way. They’re now a form of slowdown (by forcing you to go to the furthest TV from you), a way to remove stacks, and a way of turning off TVs.
### What is there to expect when learning Sadako?
Sadako has A LOT of abilities in her kit that make her stand out as a killer. Many other killers may only have at least 2 things in their kit and that's it. Sadako has way more than that and they’ll be listed here:
Mobility (Teleport) | This is her best piece of the kit. Sadako can instantaneously teleport to 1 of 7 pre-placed TVs whenever she wants. She stands out in this category because she can spam her TP. Killers like Freddy and Dredge must wait some time before being able to teleport again. Sadako does not. Although the TV itself does go on a cool-down, she still has other locations that she can TP to.
Pressure | Early game Sadako lacks any form of pressure. Her pressure shines mostly mid-late game. Her ability to pressure comes in the form of avoiding her Mori. This forces survivors to stay off important objectives just to get rid of some stacks.
Stealth | Sadako can turn invisible at intervals. A directional ring sound can reveal her location, but this doesn't happen consistently. She also gains the undetectable status effect. There is also an advanced sneak-up tech where you manifest prematurely to use the invis to sneakup.
Chase | Along with her stealth-invis is her chase-invis. Her invisibility allows her to hide her checkspots to be a few steps closer in chase. This is VERY difficult to learn, but once you fully master it, it turns all loops into 50/50s.
Mori | The mori itself is great especially if the survivor is fresh hook. You can kill someone without even hooking them. But doing it now isn’t as effective as before.
### How can I easily differentiate between certain phrases in her power?
Manifest is “uncloak”
Demanifest is “cloak”
Projecting is to “teleport”
Inexorable stare is a fancy way of saying “mori”
Manifesting can also be done automatically right after teleporting.
“Tapes” refers to the VHS tapes survivors interact with
TVs refers to the object Sadako can teleport to. Attached to the TV is the VCR, which survivors interact with.
### What are the TV Cool-downs?
45 seconds on the TV when Sadako TPs
70 seconds on the TV when a survivor retrieves/inserts a tape.
30 seconds on the Power at the start of the match.
No cooldown on the Power after projecting. So you can spam TP, as long as there are remaining TVs activated.
### Are there benefits to Demanifesting / Demanifest ?
Yes. Your terror radius turns into a 24m lullaby that gets louder based on distance.
You passively phase when within 24m meters of a survivor.
You gain the Undetectable status effect
You can project (teleport) to TVs.
You lose collision with survivors.
You retain bloodlust and chase; unlike Wraith
You’re immune to pallet stuns.
Although you do slow down to 4m/s when Manifesting. And you cannot attack.
### It’s hard to tell whether she's manifested or demanifested .
Here are some features unique to her manifested (normal) state:

Terror radius
Can M1
Her flickering is frequent (not always a constant indicator)
Now features unique to her demanifested state:
Undetectable
Lullaby (ringing sound) LOUD WARNING

She goes invisible for longer

### What happens if I take a tape?

Your screen turns into an old tv effect.
The TV that you took the tape from turns off for 70 sec.
You gain the ability to place the tape into the furthest TV from your location, in which to turn off and remove up to 3 stacks.
You gain the ability to quickly turn off other TVs, at the cost of gaining 1 stack.
Your survivor portrait turns into a distinct static. Which Sadako can see.
You become immune to the “whispers” of the TV
You remove 3 stacks of condemned after reinserting the Tape, unless you have locked condemned, which may limit this depletion based on the respective amount locked.
### Are there quirks to Sadako that make her unique to other killers?
Yes
Sadako becomes completely invisible when beyond        24m from a survivor; regardless of her state.
She’s one of the only 4.6m/s killer to have a base 24m terror radius.
She’s one of the shortest killers, Shorter than some survivors.
She’s one of a few killers to have a Mori built in their power.
She’s one of a few killers that can travel instantaneously.
She’s one of a few killers to have a limited item .
She’s the only killer to not touch the survivor. She uses telekinesis.
She’s one of a few killers to go completely invisible
She's one of a few killers that can’t get pallet stunned under certain conditions.
She's the first killer to have her base power tied towards hooks.

No. When she’s exiting, she gets a temporary lightborne effect
But you can still blind her even when she’s demanifested .

### How do I know if someone has X amount of stacks?
There are many ways of finding out.
If their portrait is red, then they have 7 stacks.
You can estimate how much they have based on the amount of VFX there is around the survivor. The more intense it is, the higher the stacks.
You’d know they gained a stack if their portraits turns into a cloudy ring; or if the cloudy ring does a faint glow .
When a survivor is hooked with the ring drawing , addon, all other survivors gain 1 stack
For each TP, everyone within 16m of you gains 1 stack, and anyone within 16m of any turned-on TV also gains a stack.

### Should I buy Sadako?
There are many reasons why you want to buy a killer. So I’ll explain some possible reasons:
Perks: Sadako has great generator perks. Call of brine being her best. Kicking a generator with this perk regresses it 25% faster. She also has Merciless storm . The last 10% of a generator continuously spawns difficult skill checks. It’s mediocre but can be paired well with killers like Doctor. And lastly there is Scourge hook: Floods of rage . It's an alright detection perk. It synergizes well for killers that can snipe, or killers with high mobility.
Gameplay: Sadako is a great killer to learn if you’re new to the game. She uses basic killer functions (M1, hold forward) And her power can greatly confuse newer survivors. Even good players find her confusing. She takes a while to fully master, but learning her basic functions can be enough to guarantee good games.
Strength/Wins: Her chase, slowdown, mobility, stealth, and mori all add up to make her a good killer. She has anti-stun and invis for chase; the survivors must transport a tape constantly, which forces them off gens; she can TP across the map instantly; she has the best stealth, and grab potential in the game; and her mori can kill survivors without even needing to hook them. If you’re looking for a killer with a high win rate, Sadako is a great choice.
### What are the timings for the passive phasing?
After manually manifesting, for a period of 4 seconds, you passively phase in and out for
.5 + .75 + .5 + .75 + .5 + .75 + .25 = 4 (the light blue number is when you go invisible)
When demanifested , for an indefinite period, you passively phase in and out for
1.5 + 1.2 + 1.5 + 1.2 + 1.5 + 1.2 + 1.5 + 1.2 + ……… so on . (Again, the light blue number
indicates when you go invisible)
After manually manifesting with Old newspaper
For a period of 4 seconds, you passively phase in and out for
.5 + 1 + .5 + 1 + .5 + .5 = 4 (you go invisible less frequently, but for longer)
After manually manifesting with Mothers mirror
for a period of 6 seconds, you passively phase in and out for
.5 + .75 + .5 + .75 + .5 + .75 + .5 + .75 + .5 = 6 (you go invisible more times)
After manually manifesting with Old newspaper and Mothers mirror
For a period of 6 seconds, you passively phase in and out for
.5 + 1 + .5 + 1 + .5 + 1 + .5 + 1 = 6
When demanifested with Reiko's Watch , for an indefinite period, you passively phase in and out for
1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5 ……….
