A simple guide to teach everyone how to loop and waste the killer time.

## #1 Intro
Are you new to DBD or ur just interested in winning most of ur chases and wasting the killer's time? I'm here to teach you how to loop structures, killer shack, rocks and trees. Firstly, you need to stop being afraid and stop crouching in the corner of the map doing nothing.

Your goal when looping is simple: make the Killer spend as much time as possible chasing you while using as few resources as possible.
## #2 Vaults
There are 3 different vaults:
 The Fast Vault - this vault is the best vault u can take while being chased because it's the quickest one and it's harder for the killer to catch you. This vault will take 0.5 seconds.
 The Medium Vault - this vault is the second best vault u can take; taking this vault will get your body more exposed and making it easier for the Killer to hit you from a distance. This vault will take 0.9 seconds. The killer can grab you from this vault action. 
 The Slow Vault - taking this vault will be slower but quiet. This vault will take 1.5 seconds. The killer can grab you from this vault action. 
Originally posted by author: If a chased Survivor rush vaults the same Window thrice during the same Chase, The Entity will temporarily block it for that Survivor for 30 seconds. Other Survivors and the Killer can still vault the Window and are unaffected by the Blocker.
Fast or Medium Vaulting is considered a rushed action  and will alert the Killer with a noise notification. This can be suppressed by using the perk Quick & Quiet  from Meg Thomas.

## #3 Tight Looping
When looping the killer you need to 'hug' the structure (hold W and stay as close as you can to the wall with camera positioned at the wall). Not doing so well result in the killer being able to catch up to you faster, decreasing your chase time.

## #7 Pallets
The vaulting speed of a window is similar to vaulting speed of a pallet.
If you wanna play safe you can predrop pallets.
Stunning a killer with a pallet will stop him from moving for 2 seconds.
The killer can grab you while vaulting a pallet.

You can reset a dropped pallet with the perk 'Any Means Necesary'. 


Pallets can be BLOCKED by the killer by using the perk Hex: Blood Favour.
[2 images]
## #8 Tips to escape Chase faster
If the killer is breaking a pallet or vaulting a window, you can flashlight/flashbang him, making him harder to find you (not if you're injured and the killer got headphones).

Don't forget, practice makes perfect. So if you wanna get better get chased by the killer and don't forget to get value from your perks. I recommend you using the perk Windows of Oportunitty for the beggining.
