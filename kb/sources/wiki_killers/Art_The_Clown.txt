# Art The Clown
Source: https://deadbydaylight.wiki.gg/wiki/Art_The_Clown (page complète via API, consultée le 27/09/2026)

Art The Clown
 or 
"The TBA"
 is one of 
2
 upcoming 
Killers
 currently featured in 
Dead by Daylight
. He will be the 45th killer.

He will be introduced as the Killer of 
Terrifier
, a Chapter 
DLC
 set to release sometime in November 2026.

He originates from the 2016 Horror franchise 
Terrifier
.

Trivia

Art The Clown
 was teased in the 
Dont Blink
 teaser, with the video featuring many elements that are associated to 
Terrifier
.

The first teaser was with Dwight's broken glasses being a reference to 
Jonathan Shaw
.

The second teaser was with a trashbag featuring limbs, which is a reference to 
Art'
s iconic trash bag.

The third and final teaser was a blow-up Clown doll.

Art The Clown
 was revealed in live action on the 10th anniversary livestream, and he was reprised by his original actor 
David Howard Thornton
.

David Howard Thornton
 took a picture with the actor that played 
Jason Voorhees
, at the cabin experience and who appeared during the livestream.

According to Mathieu Cote on a interview, 
Art The Clown
 will break records with his Mori.

Currently its unknown whether he will surpass 
The Slasher
 in the amount of ways to kill Survivors or with the gore.
