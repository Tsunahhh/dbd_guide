Overview

A member of the Celestial Echelon, The Judgment metes out divine punishment throughout the cosmos. When the people of Terra Primus aspired to godhood, The Judgment answered their sins with fiery retribution, razing their cities to the ground with pillars of divine light. So began a decades-long apocalypse that turned their paradise into a wasteland.

The Judgment
 enforces the will of The Creators with its divine light.

Its personal perks, 
Celestial Witness
, 
Hex: Under Your Thumb
 and 
Lay Waste
, allow it to track the 
Obsession
, limit and surveil Survivors with 
Haste
, and increase 
Generator
 regression.

Lore

Recovered Fragments from the Arbiters’ Book of Letters:
“Would you be counted among the heretics cleansed from this world? Or do you aspire to prove yourself to Judgment time and again, as we have proved ourselves? Then heed well these words, for their collective wisdom have saved us and, we pray, will save us still each time It returns.” 
(I Barbarians, ch. 3)
“Some of you ask, ‘What was life before Judgment?’ Those old enough to remember dare not tell, but know that the world called Terra Primus was rife with sin and heresy. The faithless built great and terrible monuments to themselves, cities reaching into the sky, ships that pierced the heavens, and unholy machines that reached across worlds. The rulers of Terra Primus elevated themselves as gods, and in their wisdom, the Celestials sent Judgment so that we may see the error of our ways and suffer for it.” 
(Truths Revealed, ch. 5)
“Do not seek to return to the old ways, for the towers and technologies of Terra Primus were heresy.” 
(Laws from Beyond, ch. 15)
“Remember the settlement of Dend. Remember Lucia the Mother, who offered up her babe to Judgment and declared “great Celestial, bear witness to my devotion!” and plunged the knife into her only child. Judgment brought its light upon the people of Dend regardless, as punishment for sacrificing the innocent.” 
(I Barbarians, ch. 11)
“Build only that which allows you to live. Sow only that which is needed to eat. To do more than this is heresy and must be punished.” 
(Laws from Beyond, ch. 1)
“Remember that even the one who built the first shrine to Judgment was burned in Its light. Of all in the encampment, the only one spared was Koenrad the Pious, who pierced his hands upon the shrine.” 
(I Barbarians, ch. 12)
“To worship is to suffer. Do not forget your worship but refrain from overindulging. The extent to which you must suffer is for you to discover. Anything more or less is heresy and must be punished.” 
(Laws from Beyond, ch. 2)
“Some of you ask of the Celestials, saying ‘What is their nature? What is the order of their echelon?’ Know that their nature is unknowable, that their order is the ultimate order. Know that Judgment is the favorite of the echelon, that It represents all that is right and just across all time and all worlds.” 
(Truths Revealed, ch. 2)
“Do not seek answers outside of Judgment, for any deviation from the will of the Gods is heresy and must be punished.” 
(Laws from Beyond, ch. 5)
“Remember Gerhardt the Blind, who tore out his own eyes as testament to the trust he placed in Judgment. Gerhardt proved himself worthy and his death from old age was a gift from Judgment. But remember also those who followed in his example and tore out their own eyes. They were burned in Judgment’s light, as punishment for their bare-faced imitation.” 
(II Barbarians, ch. 7)
“Fear is faith. Fear is love for Judgment. To live a life without fear is heresy, and must be punished.” 
(Laws from Beyond, ch. 5)
“Some of you ask, ‘Why does Stardotter the Deceiver prosper? She builds beyond what is needed and stores food and wealth. She does not worship, she seeks answers in the stars. Why do her repeated heresies go unpunished while we starve and strive to see our siblings, spouses, and children burn in Judgment’s light?’ Know that Stardotter the Deceiver lives so that we may punish her, and all who follow her. This is the greatest test of our worthiness, and Judgment permits us to live so that we may triumph over her.” 
(Truths Revealed, ch. 3)
“Look to your neighbors, and see that they are righteous in word and action. Granting mercy to heretics is itself heresy, and must be punished.” 
(Laws from Beyond, ch. 8)
“Remember Ragnar the Traitor. Once a proud hunter of heretics, she abandoned The Arbiters and sought protection under the shadow of Stardotter the Deceiver. Protection she did not find, for when Judgment returned the following year, Ragnar was destroyed in Its light.” 
(III Barbarians, ch. 6)
“Turn your ears away from those who followed the Deceiver. Ignore tales of black fog or dark shadows. Punish those who would talk of the Deceiver triumphing over Judgment. Engaging with the enemy’s lies is heresy and must be punished.” 
(Laws from Beyond, ch. 3)
“Let these be the last words of The Arbiters. Judgment has not returned in twelve winters, not since It destroyed Stardotter the Deceiver. But even though our God is gone, and the heretics who call themselves Auroran have brought us low, we will never waver in our devotion. Judgment will surely return, and we will be remembered as the ones who remained faithful. It is our grim responsibility, for our children, and for our children’s children, to fight against heresy and create a new world in Judgment’s image.” 
(Final Testament, ch. 11)

General

The Judgment
 is the first original 
Killer
 entirely designed by the community as part of The 
Grimoire
.

Prior to 
The Judgment
, The 
Doctor
 was the closest to a community-developed Killer, as his main theme was chosen by the Chinese community, drawing inspiration from a real-life figure (
Yang Yongxin
).

During the 
Ask Me Anything - About the Grimoire!
[
1
]
, Behaviour Interactive stated they considered giving 
The Judgment
 a more gender-neutral appearance, as opposed to its originally feminine concept art.

They also mentioned the possibility of giving the Killer both male and female voice lines, similar to The 
Unknown
.

Ultimately It remained feminine in appearance.

Due to an error on Behaviour's social media channels, the second-place design was mistakenly announced as the winner instead of the actual winning concept.

The Judgment
 had 3 alternative names before It's name was decided by the community.

The Apocalypse

The Exalted

The Messenger

The Judgment
 had 3 alternative perks before the final 3 were decided by the community.

Run Down the Sinner
 Which was a totem perk that gave incremental haste in chase.

Hex: The Flesh is Weak
 Which was a hex perk that would have increased the exhaustion duration.

Desolation
 Which was a perk that gave broken to survivors who  were nearby and recovered from the dying state while they were inside the killer's terror radius.

It is currently unknown if these perks will return in future chapters.

The Judgment
 is the first Killer whose Power can be directly modified by the game settings.

Despite power descriptions being reworked to instead be split into separate elements in 
Patch 10.1.0
, 
The Judgment
 still has a description in the style of pre-10.1.0 descriptions, but it cannot be seen in-game by any means.

Text for the pre-10.1.0 style description:

Expand to view content

Description

DIVINE LIGHT
Press the Power button to Charge Will of The Gods and call upon Divine Light, allowing The Judgment to control its trajectory for a moment.
Divine Light passes through all obstacles, at a slightly lower speed.
When The Judgment is ready, they can Cast Divine Light, sending forth, becoming lethal and gaining constant speed, unaffected by obstacles.
The longer Divine Light is controlled, the stronger the Cast action will be, increasing its acceleration, duration, and becoming lethal more quickly.
Anytime a Survivor is caught in a Cast Divine Light’s trajectory, if they are not a Heretic, they gain Heresy and lose a health state.
HERESY
When a Survivor is damaged by Divine Light, they gain Heresy, which makes their repairs less efficient, causing loss of progress on Good Repair Skillcheck.
Heresy will decay over time after Repenting at a Shrine of Judgment, when Liberating Heretics from Exile or when saved from the Exile.
EXILE
When standing over a Survivor affected by Heresy, The Judgment can Exile them or Kill them if they are on their second hook, gaining Zealous.
When Exiled, they gain a Hook State and must dodge Seeds of Punishment, which seek to purify the Exile of their presence. While in Exile, Survivors can use the Searcher’s Pendant to collect Exiled Souls, which grant them bonus protections when saved from the Exile by Survivors who liberate them at a Shrine of Judgment.
ZEALOUS
While Zealous, The Judgment gains the ability to quickly redirect Divine Light after casting it.
Divine Light is also larger and faster.

Memento Mori

The Judgment
 is the third Killer whose Memento Mori overrides the default Memento Mori music.

The first was The 
Trickster
's 
MiNA
 outfit and the second was The 
Slasher
 .

The Judgment
 is the seventh Killer with a base-kit ability to kill Survivors using its Power.

The Judgment
 is also the first original with the ability to 
Mini-Mori
 the Survivors, as the ability had been exclusive to a few Licensed Killers since its introduction 6 years prior.

Development

The first iteration of 
Divine Light (Formerly known as Ray of Retribution)
 used to work as a laser that would follow 
The Judgment's
 hand, but it was deemed unfun and too easy to use by playtesters.

The second and current iteration of 
Divine Light
 functions as an Area-of-Effect laser-like attack that can be moved by killer after it spawns. It is currently under playtesting and polishing.

Which is quite similar to "Pursuing Smite", an option from the grimoire before 
Ray of Retribution
 won the vote.

At some point in the early stages of development, the Killer Effect went from being a progressive effect  (0-100%) to a binary effect (on/off), to alleviate cognitive load as binary states are easier to understand, according to the developers in the 
Ask Me Anything - About the Grimoire!
.

The Judgment'
s placeholder model used The 
Spirit
's 
Ragged Burial Kimono
 as body and 
Putrid Katana
 as weapon. It lacked a head model and instead used a blue flame effect.

In a developer preview, the interaction to cast the 
Divine Light
 was jokingly named "Woe Be Upon Ye".
