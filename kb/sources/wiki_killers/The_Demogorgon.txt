Overview

The Demogorgon
 is an unidentified Killer, able to open multiple Portals and traverse between them to cover large distances. Using its Power, 
Of the Abyss
, it can detect any Survivors within a close proximity to the Portals then unleash a leaping attack to inflict damage from a distance.

Its personal Perks, 
Surge
, 
Mindbreaker
, and 
Cruel Limits
, impact the environment close to Generators and affect nearby Survivors in unexpected ways.

During the Chapter's temporary departure from 
Dead by Daylight
, these Perks were replaced with a generic variant each: 
Jolt
, 
Fearmonger
, and 
Claustrophobia
. These generic perks have been replaced with 
The Demogorgon's
 original Teachable Perks with the Chapter's return to 
Dead by Daylight
.

Lore

A blooming mouth full of needle-like teeth for a face, large, curved, razor-sharp claws, and powerful legs to pounce on victims, make The Demogorgon a frightening monster to face in any dimension. It is a nightmare of unrestrained, feral rage as it hunts down its prey and rips it to pieces, devouring every last morsel of flesh and gore, leaving nothing for scavengers. The creature is untouched by any sense of compassion or restraint. Looming over its victim, it shows no doubt or mercy, just the pure instinct of its insatiable blood lust as it delivers the deathblow. A perfect hunter, The Demogorgon is a macabre testament to the horrors lurking in the Upside Down and why it was choice pickings for The Entity.

Trivia

General

The Demogorgon
 was the first non-human Killer featured in 
Dead by Daylight
, being a creature from a parallel dimension, that entered 
Hawkins National Laboratory
.

The Demogorgon
 was the second Killer to have a unique Terror Radius and Chase Music.

The Demogorgon
 was the second Killer to not attack with an actual weapon, but instead using its claws.

The Demogorgon
 was the third Killer to break Generators and Pallets with its hands, albeit using both of them.

The Demogorgon
 was the fourth Killer to be able to teleport.

The Demogorgon
 was the tenth licensed Character to be introduced to 
Dead by Daylight
.

It was also the first license to leave 
Dead by Daylight
, before ultimately returning.

The Demogorgon
, unlike other tall Killers, does not step over vaults, but rather crawls through them.

The Demogorgon
 features a unique Hatch-closing animation.

The Demogorgon
 shares its lobby theme with 
The First
 and the 
Stranger Things
 survivors.

Development

The Demogorgon
 featured in 
Dead by Daylight
 was modelled after the Demogorgon seen in a post-credit scene of the last episode of Stranger Things' Season 3, with Netflix providing the Developers with their model.

The name 
Of the Abyss
 is a reference to 
Dungeons and Dragons - 5th Edition
, since the Dungeons and Dragons character Demogorgon appears as an enemy in an adventure titled 
"Out of the Abyss"
.

This connection is further cemented by the fact 
Dungeons & Dragons
 joined Dead By Daylight long after 
Stranger Things
 joined.

Name

The name of 
The Demogorgon
 originates from the board game 
Dungeons & Dragons
, which the main characters used as a source of inspiration to name and explain the strange happenings in Hawkins.

The same is true with 
The First
 sharing his name with 
Vecna
.

Terror Radius

The Demogorgon
 was the first Killer to have had its 
Terror Radius
 changed twice.

(PTB Version 2)

Public Test Build
