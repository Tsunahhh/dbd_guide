Overview

Merciless and unforgiving, The Executioner brings his own form of twisted justice to The Fog, tormenting Survivors with hazards around the map. Hooks are an effective tool, but nothing punishes quite like a Cage of Torment. All hope will swiftly fade the moment Pyramid Head raises his mighty blade to deliver the Final Judgment.

The Executioner
 is a map-manipulation Killer, able to torment Survivors with the hazards he creates.
Affected Survivors will be vulnerable to his special 
Hook
, the 
Cage of Atonement
 and his special Mori, 
Final Judgement
.

His personal Perks, 
Forced Penance
, 
Trail of Torment
, and 
Deathbound
, allow him to aggressively defend his prey while deceiving and tracking Survivors.

Lore

A sadistic and merciless executioner, Pyramid Head is fixated on dispensing punishment through pain.

Encumbered by the steel frame upon his head and with a hulking great blade in tow, he stalked the hellish corridors of Silent Hill, committed to a duty that no one truly understood. Where he trod, even monsters fled for the shadows, and those who crossed his path fell victim to unrestrained acts of aggression.

When his duty was complete and his presence no longer needed, he prepared for the long rest — and yet, his skills were required elsewhere. The Fog that streamed over him was somehow different than that he was accustomed to in Silent Hill, as if each wisp contained the nerves of a creature, writhing, seeking him out.

There was an unspoken agreement in that moment. The billowing cloud was an invitation to duty and sadism, and Pyramid Head, taking a step into The Fog, accepted his obligation once more.

Trivia

General

The Executioner
 was the thirteenth licensed Character to be introduced and the first video game licensed killer.

The Executioner
 was the second non-human Killer featured in 
Dead by Daylight
, being a physical manifestation of 
James Sunderland
's guilt.

The Executioner
 was the fifth Killer to have a special chase music.

The Executioner
 is the first Killer to receive a Power that affects a Survivor's Sacrifice Process.

Cages of Atonement
 are internally referred to as 
"Deathbeds"
.

The Executioner
 is the tallest Killer in the game based on absolute model-height.

The Executioner
 possesses an unused model of a tongue in the Game Files.

The Executioner's
 tongue originally appears in 
Silent Hill 2
, when he grabs 
James Sunderland
 by the throat in a boss fight.

The Executioner's
 creator, 
Masahiro Ito
, explained that it "[s]trictly speaking [is] something like a tongue.
[He] used [the] word tongue for [the sake of] convenience.
There is no way the helmet is his actual head.
Also, the bottom left of the front plate of Pyramid Head's helmet is turned up."
[
1
]

Unlike all other Killers and due to the size of his head, 
The Executioner
 does not have any animations for looking upwards or down at the ground. Instead, the position of his left hand shows whether he is looking up or down.

The Executioner
 alongside The 
Dark Lord
 is the fourth licensed killer to join 
2v8
, with its fifth iteration being based on both the 
Silent Hill
 and 
Castlevania
 chapters.

Memento Mori

The Executioner
 was the first Killer introduced with a "Mini-Mori" ability and is one of the few Killers with a base-kit ability to kill Survivors using his Power.

While technically the third Killer to have multiple Memento Mori animations, he was the first to feature two entirely distinct Moris:

His default Mori features him lifting the Survivor with his left hand, wrapping them in barbed wire and twisted steel, and finally piercing them with his 
Great Knife
.

His 
Final Judgement
 features him cleaving the Survivor in half lengthwise with an overhead swing of his 
Great Knife
.

His default Mori appears to be partially inspired by a kill animation from a boss fight in 
Silent Hill 2
, where he grabs 
James Sunderland
 by the throat.
