Overview

A stealth-focused Killer capable of approaching unseen, The Ghost Face is adept at stalking his prey. Keep tabs on each individual Survivor, patiently marking them for death – provided you can remain hidden. Lie in wait until the perfect time to strike, and down Survivors before they know what hit them.

The Ghost Face
 is a creepy Killer, able to stalk his victims and sneak up silently using his Power, 
Night Shroud
. Affected Survivors will find themselves vulnerable and oblivious to his presence and must use all of their perception and awareness to protect themselves from his approach.

His personal Perks, 
I'm All Ears
, 
Thrilling Tremors
, and 
Furtive Chase
, give him the ability to locate Survivors, defend Generators, and make his Chases unpredictable.

Lore

Danny Johnson, known as Jed Olsen by some, grabbed the newspaper from the kitchen counter: it was a week old, but his face was on the front page, grainy and sunken. It was one of those muggy afternoons in Florida when heat and humidity permeated everything in the kitchen, making him sweat while standing still. He slouched in a damp chair to read. This article had better be good—his work in Roseville had been outstanding.

GHOST FACE DISAPPEARS

June 18, 1993

At first glance, Jed Olsen was a modest and enthusiastic freelancer with experience in a variety of small newspapers. The staff at the Roseville Gazette appreciated how easy-going and honest he seemed, and so he was treated as a stranger for no more than five minutes into his interview:

"Jed quickly spotted the editor-in-chief in the room, gave him a wide smile and a firm handshake, and talked about good old American values. And that was it, he was in." —Ex-Contributor at the Roseville Gazette

Olsen never justified his erratic career path, which zigzagged between several small towns from Utah to Pennsylvania. There was no verification of his previous jobs. He had a decent portfolio plus a good attitude, and they needed a contributor right away.

THE ROSEVILLE MURDERS

Olsen had been working at the newspaper for five months when the Roseville Murders began: victims from young to old, stabbed to death in their homes. From the reports, the victims seemed chosen at random, yet the killer knew his way around in the houses. The multiple stab wounds indicated a personal motive. No traces of DNA were found. The local police were confounded: the murders were carried with fury akin to a crime of passion yet coldly premeditated.

The murderer also liked to stalk his targets. Two victims had reported being followed on their way home by a dark figure, a few days prior their death. The killer would follow them from Walleyes, a small bar in Northern Roseville, and snap pictures of them at home, while looking for a way in. He could watch the same victim for weeks, meticulously registering their habits and routines. When he felt the urge to kill, he'd visit the most vulnerable victim on his list, and break inside the house quietly.

The whole staff worked on the Roseville Murders story. Olsen was often sent to interview the family of victims and relay official statements from the police. Unknown to everyone at the time, his involvement added to the final body count.

THE GHOST FACE

Panic swelled in Roseville when Olsen produced footage of a hooded figure breaking into a house at night. The masked face, a white blur in the dark, stared at the camera for a second, before disappearing inside. “The Ghost Face Caught on Tape” was the resulting article, written by Olsen. He seemed proud of his work at the time, enjoying how the whole town feared his ghost stories.

Weeks later, Olsen left a note on his work desk and disappeared:

"I hope you liked my stories--I enjoyed bringing them to life. Don't worry, I'm not done." –Jed Olsen

The Roseville law enforcement still refuses to comment as Jed Olsen remains at large.

Danny smiled, ripping out the article from the newspaper. When the investigation had been pointing to him, he’d packed his bags and left Roseville swiftly.

He got up, the clammy seat pulling his skin. An oppressive humidity engulfed him as he entered the bedroom. Condensation dribbled on a small misted-up window as bits of cracked wallpaper hung limply. Its floral pattern was covered with gruesome photos and newspaper headlines. Danny pinned the week-old article on top of a picture of lacerated scalps. A faint pang of hunger hit him, and he wondered when he had eaten last. Was it this morning, while washing his knife and clothes? Or was it last night, after following that girl down the street? He couldn't remember clearly.

Taking a step back, he admired his work on the wall. His mind drifted, remembering all the articles he’d written, the stories he’d planned, and the scenes he’d brought to life.

A shiver ran through him. A chilling breeze transformed the bedroom's humidity into an opaque, freezing Fog. A woman shrieked. Dead leaves crunched under his feet. He smiled in anticipation.

Trivia

General

The Ghost Face
 was the first Chapter Killer to come without an accompanying Survivor or Map.

The Ghost Face
 was the first licensed Killer to have access to a selection of Customisation options in the 
Store
.

The Ghost Face
 was the seventh Killer added to 
Dead by Daylight
 wearing a mask.

Instead of climbing over vaults, 
The Ghost Face
 hoists himself through them.

The Ghost Face
 was the first Killer to have a unique running animation when in a Chase.

The Ghost Face
 was the second Killer to be able to crouch.

Despite this, 
The Ghost Face
 is the only Killer to date to be able to crouch as freely as Survivors and as an innate ability with no connection to his Power.

The Ghost Face
 is the Character with the most in-game quotes by himself, with eight in total.

The Ghost Face
 currently has the most revised Chase theme in the game, with a total of technically six different revisions of the chase theme since the 5.7.0 PTB.

This beats out The 
Demogorgon
, who had three total revisions.

The Ghost Face
 was the third licensed Killer made available in the 
2v8
 Game Mode, with its fourth iteration rewarding Players progressing through its 
Quests
 with Customisation options themed after 
Ghost Face
.

Cosmetics

Contrary to popular belief the 
Classic Ghost Face
 skin is not the same one as the Scream version, it is indeed based on the real life costume, as Scream features a custom modified version of the real life outfit with sparkles, arm tatters and pointed frills on the bottom edges.

It also features a belt that is made to make the outfit fit the user.

Some masks in the game are actually based on real life masks variants of Ghostface, those being 
Silver Ghost Face
, 
Viper Ghost Mask
, 
Devil Face'
, 
Scarecrow Ghost Face'
, 
Zombie Ghost Face
 and the 
Bleeding Ghost Face Mask
.

Devil Face
 was a mask that was made in April 2017 and was time limited to an event, and the mask eventually saw a re-release based on the version we have now.

The original mask had a satin finish, had a brighter red, horns had the same colour as the mask and darker and opaque eye mesh.

The mask would make an appearance in the fighting game 
Mortal Kombat 1
, which features the original iteration of 
Ghostface
 as a playable guest character.

Silver Ghostface
 was a limited edition mask made in 2011 to celebrate 25 years of the mask's creation.

The mask would also make an appearance in the fighting game 
Mortal Kombat 1
, which features the original iteration of 
Ghostface
 as a playable guest character.

Scarecrow Ghostface
 is based on a variant of the movie Ghostface variant of Scream as it was originally going to appear in '
Scream 4 (2011)
, before it was scrapped.

The 
Scarecrow Mask
 features larger eyes, cheeks and longer mouth compared to most of the masks.

Zombie Ghostface
 is based on a variant of the classic Ghostface mask that was released as a tie in to 
Scream 4 (2011)
.

This mask was inspired by 
Michael Myers's mask
 from 
Halloween (2007)
.

Bleeding Ghostface
 is based on a Ghostface mask variant that was made in 1997 as a tie in to 
Scream 2 (1997)
.

The real life mask is semi-transparent and has a tube that is connected to a plastic heart that you could pump fake blood into it.

Wassup Ghost Face
 is a mask that was seen in the parody movie of Scream, 
Scary Movie (2000)
, from the iconic "Wazzup!" scene.

All outfits (Aside from the classic outfit) and some masks are original creations by Behaviour Interactive being "Scorched Ghost face", The 2023 Twisted Masquerade mask and outfit, 
Metal Ghost Face
, 
Arctic Ghost Face
, and 
Ghost face gold cat mask
.

Blighted Ghost Face
 was the first original mask made by Behaviour Interactive.

It is technically not a mask in game as due to being injected with the Pustula serum by 
The Blight
, the mask is moulded with 
Danny's
 face

The 
Arctic Ghost Face Mask
 features an tear in the eyehole, and black goo on it.

If you equip the full outfit you will see that an icicle gouged the eye out.

Metal Ghost Face is the first mask to feature an angular shape and unique eye and mouth colours compared to the usual black.

The 
Gold Cat Mask
 features a angular triangular shape and smaller eyes, nose and mouth.

Ghost Face Nevermore
 is the first mask that has a unique feature over the classic mask being the crow beak.

It is the first outfit to feature claws in his gloves

It is also the first outfit whose description calls him by his alias 
Jed
.

Some of the in-game 
Ghost Face
 skins are being sold by 
Fun World
 as real life Halloween costumes.

Developer Build Leak (3.0.0)

The Ghost Face
 was accidentally leaked by the Developers when releasing the 
Patch 2.7.0
 Mid-Chapter.

Instead of releasing the live build, they released their internal 
3.0.0
 developer build, which contained an unfinished 
The Ghost Face
, aswell as unfinished 
Archives
 and 
Charms
 features, both of which released in 
Patch 3.3.1
.

This mistake was quickly noticed and reverted a couple of minutes later, but many players managed to access it before being reverted.

In this developer build, he had a different title, shortened to just 
The Ghost
.

His prestiged mask texture featured way less blood compared to the released mask.

The build featured various early animations for 
The Ghost Face
:

Memento Mori animation featured a flip phone instead of a camera.

This was also likely a yet another nod to the 
Scream
 franchise before being changed.

Despite this being changed on the live release, the Unreal Engine blueprint and the model for the prop are still named Phone in the files.

Menu animations lacked interrupt animations, his menu idle animation was also way more simple, being a 30 second idle loop where he occasionally tilted his head to the side.

Main menu idle animation has him standing up instead of crouching like he does on release.

Third person Locker grab animation is completely missing.

Various 
Legion
 animations were used as placeholders until the finished animations were put in place.

His teachable perk 
Furtive Chase
 was originally named "Simple Formula".

This was likely another reference to 
Scream
 with the iconic phrase from Randy Meeks from the original 
Scream
: 
"There's a formula to it. A very simple formula! everybody's a suspect!
".

Also featured in the build was an earlier iteration of 
The Ghost Face'
s power.

General differences:

Night Shroud
 is titled 
Shrouded Hunt
 in this build.

Night Shroud/Shrouded Hunt
 and 
Stalking
 are separate abilities, allowing 
The Ghost Face
 to stalk at all times without needing to activate 
Night Shroud/Shrouded Hunt
.

Recharging 
Night Shroud/Shrouded Hunt
 could be accelerated by stalking Survivors.

Revealing
 mechanic was extremely primitive in this build, simply requiring 
The Ghost Face
 to be in Survivor's LOS in any capacity to be considered "spotted" to start decharging 
Night Shroud/Shrouded Hunt
.

Crudely colored icons of 
Shape
's power were used as placeholders.

Lore

The Ghost Face's
 real name, as well as Roseville, the town of his first killings, are potentially inspired by 
"Danny Rolling"
, a serial killer who murdered five students within four days in 
"Gainesville"
.

He was Wes Craven's inspiration when creating the character Ghostface for the Scream movies.

Further strengthening this likely source of inspiration is the fact that the 
Viper Ghost
 Outfit description states that 
The Ghost Face
 had attended a masked party thrown by Roseville University students.

The Ghost Face's
 flying straps attached to his outfit represent his connection to The 
Entity
, according to the Developers.

In one of the Developer's streams, 
The Ghost Face
 was described as 
"a bit narcissistic"
.

Original Character / Licensed Mask

The Ghost Face
 is unique amongst the numerous licensed Killers of 
Dead by Daylight
 in that his persona is a BHVR-original creation, with only the Mask being licensed from 
Fun World
.

This also means that there is no connection to any of the movies from the 
Scream
 franchise, as 
Danny Johnson
 is not one of their multiple killers who donned the 
The Ghost Face
 alias.

Compared to the original 
Scream
 Ghost Face
, 
Dead by Daylight's
 version is a far more sinister and cunningly experienced Killer, as he has a better understanding of the risks involved in committing murder, only giving in to his bloodthirst when he knows he can get away with it.

Although not licensed from 
Scream
, he does the iconic knife wipe from the movies as a reference and tribute to it.

The knife wipe is also referenced by The 
Animatronic
, due to the 
movie version of the character
 being played by Matthew Lillard who also played Stu Macher one of the original ghostface killers.
