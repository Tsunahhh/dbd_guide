Overview

Using her Blink ability, The Nurse can teleport great distances in moments, predicting and cutting off Survivor routes. A powerful process best honed by experience, careless Blinks are punished with a wave of fatigue. The epitome of high risk, high reward, The Nurse can end chases with surgical precision.

The Nurse
 is a warping Killer, able to blink through obstacles and quickly close gaps with her Power.

Her personal Perks, 
Stridor
, 
Thanatophobia
, and 
A Nurse's Calling
, allow her to decrease Survivor abilities and track them when they are injured.

She is focused on spotting and tracking, making it harder for Survivors to hide.

Lore

Sally Smithson came to town with dreams of children's feet and laughter in a wooden home built by her husband Andrew. But life came not with smiles but with plans of destruction. Andrew worked as a lumberjack - a job with its perils. One day, Andrew's foreman had to pay Sally a visit, forever changing her life. She was alone.

Without food on the table and no other option, Sally had to find a way, but the only employment she could get was at the Crotus Prenn Asylum. Nobody sought employment there unless they were in dire need. Just like Sally. Without any education, she started at the bottom, doing the hard night shift.

Over the years, her mind had reached its limits, two decades of seeing horrid things that violate the eyes. Memories that are re-played every night. Being abused verbally and physically, by people without limits. Sally saw insanity from the outside, just to catch it herself.

Finally she could not take it any more and concepts of purification emerged inside her. She did what she felt was necessary. As the morning staff arrived one day in September - they found over fifty dead patients, lifeless, in their bed along four staff members, also dead.

Only Sally had survived the night, but her mind was gone, rocking back and forth non-stop. Exactly what happened is only known by her, but it seems that some of them had been choked as they had marks around their necks. They got her into an ambulance, but that ambulance never reached the hospital. It was found crashed in a nearby wood, all the staff dead and Sally nowhere to be found.

Trivia

General

The Nurse
 was the first female Killer.

The Nurse
 was the first Killer to be introduced as a 
DLC
.

Her 
DLC
 was later incorporated into the base game.

In The 
Entity
's 
Realm
, 
The Nurse
 shares her home turf with The 
Clown
 in the 
Crotus Prenn Asylum
.

The Nurse
 was the first Killer to use telekinesis to perform various interactions.

She is also the first Killer to float rather than walk.

She remained the only Killer to do so for nearly eight years until the addition of The 
Lich
 and The 
Dark Lord
 in his 
Vampire Form
.

The Nurse
 has the lowest default 
Movement speed
 of all Killers to balance her ability to teleport through obstacles, walls, and even floors.

However, this also makes her entirely dependent on using her Power for any sort of viable mobility in chases.

Before the standardisation of the 
Lunge Attack
, 
The Nurse
 had the shortest one amongst all Killers, due to its reach being tied to her base 
Movement speed
.

The Nurse
 was one of two Killers directly affected by the 
Lightburn
 mechanic before its deprecation due to her power's interaction with the 
Spirit World
, alongside The 
Wraith
.

Artwork from the 
Tragic Love Opera Collection
 heavily implies a romantic relationship between her and The 
Wraith
.

The description for The 
Wraith
's 
The Angel of Music
 outfit supports this, stating that he is "compelling the grieving 
Nurse
 to find her voice again".

Teleportation

The Nurse
 was the first Killer able to teleport.

She is the only Killer with an unconditional activation teleport.

She screams in pain and sighs with relief every time she uses her power, as the teleportation process itself is physically agonizing for her.

She leaves a trail of glowing dots and black smoke after teleporting.

Her power references several other games that feature teleportation abilities also named 
"Blinking"
.

Design

The Nurse
 was hinted at by the Developers to have been inspired by the nurses featured in the 
Silent Hill
 franchise.

The Nurse
 was the first Killer introduced who was notably shorter in comparison to the original Killer trio (The 
Trapper
, The 
Wraith
, and The 
Hillbilly
).

The Nurse
 does not produce audible footsteps, though her dress creates a faint rustling noise when she moves.

In the game's earlier days, 
The Nurse
 was one of the very few Killers whose breathing was not distorted in some way.

She makes a rather human-like wheeze, which sounds stressed, as if her throat is closed up.

This is possibly due to her suffering from 
Stridor
, an affliction that causes a high-pitched wheezing sound resulting from disrupted airflow, likely due to her burns.

This medical condition is also the namesake of her Perk 
Stridor
.

The Nurse
 was "bat-shit crazy" when she strangled one of her patients at Crotus Prenn Asylum, which made her more susceptible to The 
Entity
's corruption, according to the Developers during one of their Weekly Dev Streams.
