# Frank Stone
Source: https://deadbydaylight.wiki.gg/wiki/Frank_Stone (page complète via API, consultée le 27/09/2026)

Franklin Norman "Frank" Stone
 or 
"The Champion"
 is one of 
2
 upcoming  
Killers
 in 
Dead by Daylight
.  He will be the 46th killer.

He will be introduced in 
The Casting of Frank Stone
 a Chapter 
DLC
 set to release sometime in March 2027.
He originates from the 2024 Supermassive 
Dead by Daylight
 spinoff game 
The Casting of Frank Stone
.

Trivia

The Champion
 was originally going to be a legendary skin for The 
Trapper
, but due to community backlash the skin was cancelled and 
Frank
 became his own killer instead.
[
1
]

The Champion
 is the first original character to not be originated in the base game, instead coming from 
The Casting of Frank Stone
, a 
Dead by Daylight
 spinoff developed by Supermassive Games.

The Champion's
 original 1963 mask is an wearable mask for The  
Trapper
 as part of the Deluxe Edition of the game.
