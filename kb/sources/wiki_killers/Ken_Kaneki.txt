Lore

Ken Kaneki was a gentle and kind-hearted boy who lived a simple life, studying literature at Kamii University, before a chance encounter turned his life upside down.

He and his friend Hide frequented the café Anteiku, where one of the regulars caught his eye: Rize Kamishiro. She shared his passion for reading, and he spotted her even reading the same novel as him. He worked up the courage to ask her on a date. While walking her home, Rize showed her true colors: those of a ghoul known as The Binge Eater. She feasted on his flesh, nearly killing him, until a freak construction accident caused her demise, and nearly caused his as well.

To save his life, doctors transplanted Rize's organs into Kaneki. He survived the surgery, but something was wrong... food lost its flavor, and strange cravings dogged him. The life-saving surgery had made him part-ghoul.

Confused and afraid, he followed his instincts into an alleyway where he ran into Touka Kirishima, one of the staff members at Anteiku. Shocked at his transformation, she brought him back to the café. He was surprised to learn that the staff at Anteiku were ghouls. They took him under his wing, helping him adapt to his new reality. He learned how to fight with his newfound powers, how to deal with his urges to kill and eat human flesh, and how to stay one step ahead of the CCG.

His adaptation to his new life was cruelly interrupted when he was kidnapped and subjected to unprecedented physical and mental torture at the hands of a particularly cruel ghoul named Jason. His captor's exercises of control and profound violence were so diabolical that they broke Kaneki's mind, stripped him of what once made him human. His sense of self was shattered, and Rize stepped into the empty space, encouraging him to take control in his own life.

And control is what he took, paying back Jason's violence in kind. He broke free and battled his captor. The torture made him numb to pain, and his shattered psyche left him no space for hesitation. He defeated Jason after a short battle, and he devoured him.

He had been dragged into a nightmare a tormented boy, but he walked out a powerful ghoul. He considered what this would mean as he pushed his way through the heavy doors, and barely noticed as a black fog pooled around his feet. He walked out, confident that the torture was over. But as he observed his surroundings, he realized that perhaps it had only begun.

Trivia

General

The Ghoul
 is the first character to be added to come from a Manga/Anime media that isn't a cosmetic for an existing character.

Ken Kaneki
 also marks the first time in Dead By Daylight history where the killer is the main protagonist of the series.

The Ghoul
 is the first Killer to date who does not have weapon that is one of his three Cosmetic slots.

In-game, he uses his Kagune (the tentacles) for both 
Basic Attacks
 and 
Special Attacks
.

While there are others Killers without a weapon as such — like 
The Nemesis
 using his fist or 
The Dark Lord
 his hand — they at least featured that body part as a weapon Cosmetic, whereas 
The Ghoul
 does not.

His cosmetic slots mirror that of survivors, having one for his head, torso and legs.

The Ghoul
 Lore outlines the events of the first season of the 
Tokyo Ghoul
 anime.

His appearance, however, reflects the second season.

He would eventually receive the outfit from where he is taken from the final episode of the anime.

The finger-cracking 
Kaneki
 does in some animations (e.g. idling in the menu, cool-down phase after a 
Basic Attack
), is a habit that he mirrored from 
Yamori
 after the latter tortured him in 
Tokyo Ghoul
.

The Ghoul
 is the first killer to feature a quick time event in their gameplay.

When initiating a grab attack, the player has the opportunity to perfectly time an input to be rewarded with an extension to their 
Enraged Mode Countdown.

There is no penalty for failing the quick time event.

The Ghoul
 is the first Killer to date with a unique Power Token removal feature upon breaking Pallets normally.

When 
The Ghoul
 breaks a Pallet while 
Kagune Leap
 is 
not on cool-down
, it enters a cool-down of the equivalent of 1 Power Token use (or 2 while in Enraged Mode).

This was meant to alleviate a problematic tactic where 
The Ghoul
 could quickly catch up to Survivors having little to no time to approach the nearest resource, although this system is flawed and can be worked around by breaking a Pallet right before 
Kagune Leap
 leaves its cool-down.

When using the cosmetic set 
"Awakened Ghoul"
, 
Kaneki's
 voice becomes clearer, since it removes his mask.

The Ghoul's
  mori is a reference to 
Yamori's
 s defeat in the season final of season 1, where Kaneki stabs both of his hands, restraining him before he devoured his Kagune.

The associated achievement name 
"Remember Who I Am"
 is a direct reference to the last line of the lyrics in the opening song of Season 1, 
Unravel
.

Adaptation from Source

According to Dave Richards, Creative Director of 
Dead by Daylight
, their reimagining of 
Ken Kaneki
 had to undergo a drastic change in personality to suit his role as a Killer:
"Rather than making him a Survivor, as his moral compass in the series may suggest, we felt it would be more interesting to really lean into the horror and present an alternate reality where Kaneki became exactly what he feared.
While such a drastic change to Ken Kaneki's personality was necessary to suit his role as a Killer, it allowed the team to delve into a Ghoul’s powers and take them to their darkest possible conclusion. Luckily, the Tokyo Ghoul Production Committee was on board for the story we wanted to tell, sharing our excitement to see this terrifying take on their protagonist."
