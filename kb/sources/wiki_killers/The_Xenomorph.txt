Overview

A relentless specimen from a distant planet, 
The Xenomorph
 is vicious, agile, and cunning.

Its personal Perks, 
Rapid Brutality
, 
Alien Instinct
, and 
Ultimate Weapon
, allow it to cause nearby Survivors to scream, gain Haste after basic attacks, and see Auras of faraway Survivors.

Lore

A perfect organism, born of violence, designed to kill.

The Xenomorph emerged from the chest cavity of a host named Kane and sought cover in the shadows of the Nostromo. As it shed its skin, the Xenomorph came to understand its environment. Using the ship’s air ducts, it killed its prey one by one, until only the human Ripley remained.

Desperate for survival, Ripley destroyed the Nostromo and sought refuge in an escape vessel. But the Xenomorph’s instincts gave it the edge. As the escape vessel floated through space, the Xenomorph lay in wait, watching its prey.

Just as it was went in for the kill, the vessel’s airlock opened and the Xenomorph was ejected into the cold vacuum of space.
All was lost. The Xenomorph was close to death. And then, in an instant, it disappeared in a cloud of black fog.

Trivia

General

The Xenomorph's
 four-legged mode of locomotion was originally called 
"Runner Mode"
 on the 
Public Test Build
 of its Release Patch.

The mode was renamed to its current 
"Crawler Mode"
 for release, due to some Players reasonably believing 
"Runner Mode"
 to refer to its two-legged gait instead.

The Xenomorph's
 Mori is a reference to 
Alien vs. Predator (2004)
, where a Xenomorph kills a Predator called Chopper.

The Xenomorph
 is unable to hear the 
Hatch
 when inside the tunnels, so Players should not attempt searching for it that way.

The Xenomorph
 is the first killer that carries Survivors by holding them at their necks, rather than carrying them over its shoulder.

The Xenomorph
 is currently the first of two killers featuring Killer-specific 
Hooks
, rather than Realm-specific ones.

That behavior, however, is not a first in 
Dead by Daylight's
 history, as all 
Hooks
 used to be Killer-specific when the Game first released and until the introduction of 
The HALLOWEEN® Chapter
.

The Xenomorph's
 hooks are the same ones from 
Nostromo Wreckage
.

The Xenomorph's
 Tunnels are a reference to how it traversed the Nostromo, mainly through its ventilation shafts in the original movie. The visual design of the tunnels reflects the bio-matter covering structures 
The Xenomorphs
 incorporate into their nests.

The Xenomorph's
 tail attack is likely a reference to the game 
Aliens vs Predator (2010)
 where Xenomorphs use their tails as attack in that game.

The Xenomorph
 has a skull underneath its dome that is visible in promotional art, but not in-game.

The Xenomorph'
s official designation 
Xenomorph XX121
 was first introduced in the 2014 book 
Alien: The Weyland-Yutani Report
 by S.D. Perry, and was made fully official in 
Alien: Romulus
.

The term 
"Xenomorph"
 first emerged from 
Aliens
.

The Xenomorph'
s scientific name internecivus raptus first appeared in the Alien Quadrilogy DVD and was made official in the 
Marvel Comics'
s  run of Alien.

It roughly translates from Latin to "murderous thief".

The Xenomorph
 was the seventh licensed killer added to the 2v8 gamemode

Early Development

According to the developers 
The Xenomorph
 was originally going to have wall crawling in its power, but was scrapped and replaced with the tunnel system.

Lore

This is the same Xenomorph from the first Alien movie (Kane's son/Big Chap), and in Dead By Daylight's lore, it claims that it would have died in space if it wasn't for 
The Entity
. However in 
Alien: Romulus
, Kane's son survived and was taken by Weyland-Yutani, and wrecked havoc in the Renaissance space station, before being gunned down by the remaining security team, thus making him the catalyst of the movie's events before they happened. His massacre on the station is seen in the prequel comic of Alien: Romulus which takes place before the movie's events.

As with other Killers such as 
The Shape
 or 
The Cenobite
, the Xenomorph is from the first movie only, and not its continuities.
