Overview

The Shape lurks in the distance, patiently biding his time. His evil builds while stalking Survivors, fuelling him with malevolent power. As the Trial progresses, The Shape evolves from a quiet menace to an unstoppable juggernaut capable of shredding through even the most resilient teams.

The Shape
 is a haunting Killer who stalks Survivors to feed his power.

His personal Perks, 
Save the Best for Last
, 
Play with Your Food
, and 
Dying Light
, select one Survivor to become his 
Obsession
 and will trigger deadly effects based on their status.

Lore

Some humans are simply bad seeds. Seeds infused with a distilled and pure form of evil.

Michael Myers is one of those seeds. He had no issues with causing the pain of others. Instead, it was exactly what he sought. But even life can be tough on those with minds filled with terror. The difference is just how one goes about to solve those problems. For Michael, he had to kill to find some inner peace. As he took his sister’s life, the police found a silent boy dressed as a clown at the scene.

When one stumbles upon a growing fire, one does not pour gasoline on it. But this was an action taken by officials that had no idea how it would shape this demon in the boy's body. Sending Michael to a mental institution was a feeble attempt to save the child. Unsuccessful therapy and nightly screams just made him even more introverted and deranged.

People hoped that Michael Myers would end up a parenthesis, soon to be forgotten and buried, a failure that soon were to rot away. But then...he escaped.

Trivia

General

The Shape
 was the first Killer to be introduced as a paid 
DLC
 and as a licensed Character.

The Shape
 was the first Killer to feature a Power gauge.

The Shape
 was the second Killer to wear a face mask, the first being 
Trapper
.

The Shape
 is the only Killer with the ability to modify 
Pallet
 stun recovery speed and 
Lunge Attack
 duration during the Trial with his base-kit Power.

The Shape
 is one of the two killers who makes a sound effect when selected from the lobby or the 
Store
, the other being The 
Nightmare
.

In 
The Shape's
 case, the leitmotif of 
Halloween
 can be heard.

He was the third licensed Killer to leave with the retirement of the 
Halloween
 Chapter.

The other two licensed Killers to leave were The 
Demogorgon
 and The 
Cenobite
. The first one has since returned along with its Chapter.

The Shape's
 in-game name is a reference to the original 1978 
Halloween
 Movie: during the credits, the adult Michael Myers (while masked) is credited as 
"The Shape"
.

Cosmetics

Default Outfit
 originates from 
Halloween (1978)
, and is worn by Michael after escaping the 
Smith's Grove Sanitarium
 throughout the rest of the film.

Escaped Patient
 originates from 
Halloween (1978)
, and is worn by Michael as he escapes 
Smith's Grove Sanitarium
.

Michael is maskless while wearing the gown in the movie.

Differences from the Movies

In the movie, the mask is different, with a lower hairline. He is much shorter, and he can be seen wearing Green Vietnam Combat Jungle Boots as well as his coveralls being muted tex green.

In-game, the mask has a higher hairline, he is taller, and wears standard boots. He also has a much bigger body build.

His bigger stature has led many players to falsely believe that this version of the character is based on the version from the 2007 and 2009 
Halloween
 and 
Halloween II
 remakes.

Memento Mori

The Shape
 was the first Killer with a unique way to kill Survivors.

The Shape
 was the first Killer to have two Memento Mori animations, both animations changing only the start.

When Killing a Survivor in 
Evil Incarnate
, 
The Shape
 grabs them from behind and turns them around before lifting them.

Killing a Survivor in the 
Dying State
 via 
Evil Incarnate
, 
Perks
 or 
Offerings
 has 
The Shape
 lean down and grab the Survivor before lifting them.

The Shape's
 Memento Mori is likely a reference to how he kills Bob Simms in the 1978 film.

He dashes out of a closet, grabs Bob, lifts him and stabs him through the torso with his knife.

In an unknown Patch, the animation was changed to feature 
The Shape
 tilting his head, similar to how he tilted his head in the 1978 film after killing Bob.

This animation change wasn't applied to the standing grab Memento Mori.

Up until 
Patch 9.2.0
, 
The Shape
 was the first Killer that could Mori the Survivors regardless of how many times they had been hooked in a Trial.

Additionally with Patch 9.2.0, 
The Shape
 became one of the few Killer that could Mori the Survivors with his Power. He is the only one of these Killers whose Power does not grant the Mini-Mori.

Portrait

Like all Killers, 
The Shape
 received a new Character Portrait with 
Patch 7.1.0
.
However, the current version, released with 
Patch 7.2.0
, was the second iteration of that Portrait due to some grievances the Community had with the previous iteration:

The first iteration featured 
The Shape
 holding up his knife and staring at it, which many Community members deemed 
"too comical"
.

The second iteration is now more reminiscent of his pose featured in the cover art of 
The HALLOWEEN® Chapter
, which in itself is a reference to the film's poster.

Terror Radius

The Shape
 was the first and for a long time the only Killer to feature a unique 
Terror Radius
 and 
Chase Music
.

All of the music featured in the game are renditions of tracks from the original 1978 
Halloween
 OST.

The Menu jingle as well as the Tier-up sound cues are based on snippets from the 
"Halloween Main Theme"
.

The Terror Radius/Chase Music is an original rendition of 
"The Shape Stalks"
.

The Tier-up Warning sound cue is based on 
"The Hedge"
.

He can switch between 3 different 
Terror Radii
 during the Trial.

Voice Actor

The Shape
 is voiced by Frédéric Poirier, who at that time worked as the Senior Sound Designer on the Game.

He also voiced 
Felix Richter
.
