Overview

A visionary as brilliant as he is ruthless, Albert Wesker’s strategic mind is unrivalled. While some plans require intellect, others call for brute force. Rush forward to seize Survivors in your grasp and give them the gift of Uroboros – a necessary step on the path to evolution.

Albert Wesker
 is a brilliant and ruthless mastermind infused with the Power of Uroboros.

His personal Perks, 
Superior Anatomy
, 
Awakened Awareness
, and 
Terminus
, allow him to vault faster, see other Survivors' Auras while carrying one, and cause Survivors to be permanently 
Broken
 when the Exit Gates are powered.

Lore

Albert Wesker was a high-ranking official in the Umbrella Corporation. While he made great strides with Umbrella, his ambition drove him to betray the corporation. Believing himself the best test subject, he exposed himself to the Prototype virus. Now with superhuman strength, Wesker faked his death and went underground with a new plan: expose the world to Uroboros, a biological weapon that would eliminate the weak and leave only the strong.

Wesker began to put his plan to action in Africa but was confronted by members of the Bioterrorism Security Assessment Alliance at a hangar stocked with missiles carrying Uroboros. Determined to be at the vanguard of evolution, he once again made himself a test subject. Exposing himself to Uroboros, he began to evolve in ways he could have never imagined. But as he felt his cells change, a strange black fog seeped into the hangar and filled his vision. When the fog finally dissipated, he found himself in another world surrounded by lesser beings that deserved to be put out of their misery.

Trivia

General

The Mastermind
 is one of the most normal-looking Killers in 
Dead by Daylight
, with no initially visible physical alterations to him other than his glowing eyes, which he hides behind his sunglasses most of the time.

The Mastermind
 was the first Killer with a Power that allows him to immediately grab Survivors that is separate from a basic grab.

The Mastermind
 was the first Killer with the ability to throw Survivors as a means of damaging them.

The Mastermind
 was the second Killer with an ability that allows them to vault faster than other Killers by using their power (i.e. Special-vault), the first being The 
Legion
.

Up until 
Patch 8.6.0
, 
The Mastermind
 was the only killer with a Terror Radius of 
40 metres
.

The Mastermind
 was the first Killer to be referred to by his real name in the Killer Selection Menu rather than by a descriptive type or his Killer alias.

The Mastermind
, alongside 
Nemesis T-Type
, was the second licensed Killer made available in the 
2v8
 Game Mode, with its third iteration themed after the two 
Resident Evil
 Chapters in 
Dead by Daylight
.

Animations and Model

 running animation is a reference to Wesker's running dash attack from 
Resident Evil 5
.

The way 
The Mastermind
 swings his knife is a reference to the way the characters swing their knife in 
Resident Evil
 games.

The Mastermind's
 default knife is the knife he uses when he is playable in the mercenaries mode of 
Resident Evil 5
.

The Mastermind's
 Mori is a reference to both the first confrontation between 
Chris Redfield
 and himself in 
Resident Evil CODE: Veronica
, and 
Excella Gionne's
 death in 
Resident Evil 5
.

When stunned, 
The Mastermind's
 glasses may fall off, resulting in him pulling out another pair from his coat.

The Mastermind's
 pose in his bio is a reference to one of his artwork from 
Teppen'
, which also originated from 
The Mastermind's
 render from 
Resident Evil 5
.

The same pose from 
Teppen
 appears in one of 
The Mastermind's
 menu animations.

The title 
The Mastermind
 is likely in reference to 
Resident Evil: Resistance
, an assymetrical game where one player takes the role of the Mastermind, and must stop the Survivors from reaching the goal, by the means of various traps and B.O.W.s

Though Albert was never made playable in Resistance, his sister, Alex, was.

Lore

Wesker
 was taken by the The Entity during the final level of Resident Evil 5.

Wesker's ability to use Uroboros causes a disconnect from the game's canon. As Wesker's body was heavily mutated by the virus, whilst 
Dead by Daylight's
 Wesker retains his normal form.

According to his 
Blighted Wesker
 Cosmetic, 
The Mastermind
 is the only Killer to inject the 
Blight Serum
 into himself, as opposed to 
The Blight
 administering them.

Voice Actor

Despite some initial beliefs and theories pointing to 
Albert Wesker's
 original voice actor from ten 
Resident Evil
 games; Including '
Resident Evil 5'
, which the iteration is based on, D.C. Douglas, reprising his role for 
Dead by Daylight
, he himself confirmed that someone else stepped into his shoes for 
Dead by Daylight
, and wished the new voice actor best of luck taking on the role of 
Albert Wesker
.

It was ultimately revealed that actor and voice actor Connor Fogarty had taken on the role of voicing 
Albert Wesker
 for 
Dead by Daylight
 in collaboration with The Monster Factory Inc.

Voice-Lines

The Mastermind
 was the third Killer in the Game to actively speak in Trials.

All of his voice lines originate from 
Resident Evil 5
.

Keeping with their infamous rivalry, 
The Mastermind
 has special voice lines when interacting with 
Chris Redfield
.

The Mastermind
 was the first Killer to get voice lines when finding any Survivors in lockers.

Before him, only 
The Nemesis
 had special voice-lines for S-T-A-R-S Survivors.
