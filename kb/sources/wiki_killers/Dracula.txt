The Dark Lord, Dracula
, is the embodiment of darkness and tragedy.

His personal Perks,

,

Dominance

Lore

Inconsolable grief metamorphosed into madness after the death of Dracula's beloved. He sought out the fabled Crimson Stone as a way to cheat death, but death trapped his soul inside the stone, granting him eternal life as a vampire. Vlad Tepes Dracula was now the Dark Lord. He slept by day and hunted in the eventide, seeking out the living and stealing their lifeblood. He built a castle in Romania shielded with sinister magic and turned many of the local villagers into the living dead. Dracula's Castle became a symbol of terror and despair. His power seemed unassailable and many perished trying to defeat him as he sowed endless chaos, suffering, and death.

In 1476 Trevor Belmont of the Belmont Clan ended Dracula's reign with the Vampire Killer. But the undead never stay dead for long and Dracula was resurrected more than once over the centuries to continue his quest for revenge.

Last resurrected in 1999, Dracula raised an army of the undead. It took another descendant of the Belmont Clan to defeat him: Julius Belmont. In the savage clash - known as the Battle of 1999, Julius Belmont took the fight to Dracula's Castle and battled the Dark Lord with the same enchanted whip his distant ancestors wielded. Most believe Dracula suffered a true death. A final death. But Dracula didn't die that night. As the Vampire Killer tore the flesh from his bones, a strange fog rolled across the ancient stone floor. Dracula felt the icy fingers of something or someone beckoning him. The Fog engulfed him and transported him to a place where he could continue his dark vengeance against humanity.

Trivia

General

The file name of the icon that used to pop up as a tooltip during the loading screen for the first few times a Player encountered 
The Dark Lord
 suggests that he could have been named 
The Shapeshift
 at an earlier point in development.

The Dark Lord
 is only the third Killer since The 
Nurse
 who floats and glides across the floor instead of walking, given that he is in his 
Vampire Form
.

In a similar manner to her using telekinesis to interact with Props, 
The Dark Lord
 uses pyrokinesis instead of his own hands.

The Developers did not want 
The Dark Lord
 to use his legs to vault through 
Windows
 as that would be "beneath him" and for "lowly humans" only, prompting them to create the misty husk form with which he phases through them.

The Design of 
Dead by Daylight's
 
Dracula
 originates from the 1997 Action RPG 
Castlevania: Symphony of the Night
, though his origin game is the original 1986 Action-Platform game 
Castlevania
.

The Dark Lord
 is the first Killer to have miniature power icons next to his power icon which are used for "ability readying" and "ability casting" as well as Vampire, Wolf, and Bat icons when readying Transform.

In addition, 
The Dark Lord
 has even smaller icons for ability cooldown when not transformed into the form that uses that ability.

The Dark Lord
 is the second Killer to use a third-person camera since The 
Good Guy
.

However, 
The Dark Lord
 only uses it in 
Wolf Form
 or 
Bat Form
, with his default 
Vampire Form
 using the regular first-person camera for Killers.

The Dark Lord
 alongside The 
Executioner
 is the fifth licensed killer to join 2v8, with its fifth iteration being based on both the 
Silent Hill
 and 
Castlevania
 chapters.

Castle Easter Egg

Trials with 
The Dark Lord
 can feature 
Dracula's Castle
 as a visual Easter Egg.

The 
Castle
 looms over the Trial Grounds, situated on an inaccessible floating island beyond the border walls of the Map.

The 
Castle
 is present in all Trials that take place on an original Map that is not associated with another license.
Trials occurring on the latter do not feature the 
Castle
.

Chase Theme

The Chase Theme of 
The Dark Lord
 references his boss fight theme 
"Nothing to Lose"
 from the original NES version of 
Castlevania (1987)
.

The Dark Lord
 's skins are taken from his appearances through 
Castlevania
.

 is taken from his appearance from

.

Castlevania: Dracula X
 the Super Nintendo Entertaintment System (SNES) reimagining of 
Rondo of Blood
.

This skin mentions the events of 
Dracula X/Rondo of Blood
 and 
Richter Belmont
.

.

This outfit references 
Simon Belmont
.

Castlevania: Circle of the Moon
.

This was the first Visceral featured in 
The Rift
 and also the first one to have a recoloured 
Deep Rift
 version.

Power References

A good part of 
Vampiric Shift
 references moves 
Dracula
 performs during boss fights in various other media that he is a part of:

The 
Hellfire Spell
 is used in the boss fight between 
Dracula
 and 
Trevor Belmont
 in 
Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse (1989)
 and a move in his first phase in 
Super Smash Bros. Ultimate (2018)
.

The 
Wolf Form

Dracula's
 third phase in the video game 
Castlevania: The Dracula X Chronicles (2007)

The 
Bat Form
 references 
Dracula's
 many uses of it as a teleportation ability, though it is most reminiscent of the one featured in the 1987 arcade game 
Haunted Castle
, the second installment of 
Castlevania
 arcade games.

Memento Mori

The 
Memento Mori
 references a scene from 
Castlevania: Symphony of the Night
 in which 
Dracula
, sitting on his throne, throws and destroys his glass while saying the infamous line: 
"What is a man? A miserable little pile of secrets."

Voice Actor

The Dark Lord
 is voiced by 
Crispin Freeman
, a Californian voice actor who has voiced several Characters in the 
Castlevania
 franchise, including 
Dracula/Mathias Cronqvist
, and 
Hector
.

Voice-Lines

The Dark Lord
 is the eighth Killer to actively speak during Trials.

As his archnemesis, 
The Dark Lord
 has several voice-lines specific to encounters with 
Trevor Belmont
, both in the Lobby and during the Trial.

Voice-Line References

The Dark Lord
 has several unique voice-lines for when his nemesis, 
Trevor Belmont
, his son, 
Alucard
, and his reincarnation, 
Soma Cruz
, join the lobby, as well as when he kills them with 
Memento Mori
 or when he finds them hiding inside a 
Locker
, the most of any Killer that has special voice-lines for specific Survivors.

Towards 
Trevor
, it is clear that 
The Dark Lord
 harbours but ill feelings and disdain, whereas towards 
Alucard
, he expresses regret and loss, but also disappointment. With 
Soma
, he expresses bewilderment, curiosity, and disdain.

Several of his lines directed to 
Soma
 directly reference his nature as Dracula's reincarnation, with the line 
"You thought you could eclipse me... now die!"
 referencing the eclipse that begins the events of 
Aria of Sorrow (2003)

Several of 
The Dark Lord's
 voice-lines reference voice-lines from other 
Castlevania
 games, specifically 
Symphony of the Night (1997)
, 
Rondo of Blood (1993)
 and 
Curse of Darkness (2005)
.
